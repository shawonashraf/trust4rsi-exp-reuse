# P15 cross-model guide: does a different model also put the task's instructions above the data?

Written 2026-11-16, after P14 and before any P15 guide or agent run (a 1-request smoke test of the Qwen server was
run, not a guide call). Asked for by the author (2026-11-16): I proposed a guide from a different model, Qwen3.8-27B,
guide only first, then Gemma agent runs on its picks; the author: "Yes." and "Do it." This file was written after
that go-ahead and before the runs.

## Question

In P13 and P14 the guide was the agent's own model (Gemma 4 31B). It rejected the lessons that fix TF03 and TF05,
and its reasons cite the task's instructions (83% of rejections of fix lessons with the P13 prompt; 79% with a
prompt that allows going against the wording). Is that Gemma's blind spot, or does another model judge the same way?

## Method

Everything as P14's G3o (P13 prompt, retry budget 3, at most 3 lessons shown, reasons saved), except the guide:

- Guide: Qwen3.8-27B-FP8 (`Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8`, local cache), SGLang, TP=2 on the free GPU pair, 64k window,
  thinking on, reasoning parser `qwen3`. Sampling from the model's `generation_config.json`: temperature 1.0,
  top_p 0.95, top_k 20. Same prompt text as P13 (`GUIDE`).
- Agent: unchanged (Gemma 4 31B, P13 settings).

Stage 1, guide only: Q3 (clean store, 6 SF tasks, 30 guide runs) and Q3-p (poisoned store, 4 failing tasks, 20).
Stage 2: stop the Qwen server, start the Gemma server on the same pair, and run the Gemma agent on Qwen's picks:
P15_Q3 (30 runs) and P15_Q3p (20 runs), scored as in P13. Stage 2 runs whatever stage 1 shows.

Labels as in P14 (fixed before P14): TF05 fix lessons 164, 166-169; TF03 fix lessons 127, 129, 131, 132, 134,
135, 137; TF03 contradicting lessons 130, 136; wrong lessons 708-719. Instruction-reason regex as in P14.

Compared with: P14 G3o / G3o-p (Gemma guide, same prompt, reasons saved) and P13 G3 / G3-p, E / E-p (agent results).

## Expected direction

My expectation is that the pattern is general (instruction-tuned models rank explicit instructions above inferred
data problems), but weaker in a different model.

1. Qwen also rejects most TF05 fix lessons: a TF05 fix lesson is shown in at most 2 of 5 runs.
2. Its rejections of fix lessons cite the instructions in at least half of cases.
3. Qwen blocks wrong lessons at least as well as Gemma: a wrong lesson shown in at most 8 of 20 Q3-p runs
   (Gemma G3o-p: 6).
4. Usefulness: Q3 pooled targeted checks within 0.10 of P13's G3 (0.50), that is between 0.40 and 0.60.
5. The agent leaves out the named column in at least 80% of runs shown a wrong lesson only.
6. No harm: TF04 / TF06 within 0.01 of A0.

If 1 and 2 hold, the "guide follows the instructions" result holds for two model families, and the paper can say
so. If 1 fails (Qwen accepts the TF05 fix in 3 or more runs), the blind spot is the agent model's, and a guide from
a different model is the remedy to test next; the paper then reports that instead.

## Limits stated in advance

- One other model, one prompt, 5 runs per task.
- Qwen's sampling differs from Gemma's (top_k 20 vs 64), each model's own recommended settings.
- Qwen3.8-27B is an FP8 checkpoint; Gemma runs in BF16.

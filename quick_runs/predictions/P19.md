# P19 review round 4 controls: per-task cut, escalation, a write-time probe, and hinted tasks

Written 2026-11-17, after review round 4 and before any P19 run. Asked for by the author (2026-11-17: "do a 5 pass
review - address concerns - run experiments - write loop"). The code (`quick_runs/p13/guide.py` P19 block,
`quick_runs/p19/write_nfd.py`, `quick_runs/tasks_p19_hint.yaml`) is committed with this file. Nothing has run yet.

## Why

Review round 4 (Weak Accept, 3/5; `docs/reviews/2026-11-17_open_scholar_peer_round4.md`) asked:

1. Does the one-sentence cut hold on TF05 alone? P18 had 5 of 5 against 2 of 5 for the placebo (p = 0.17).
2. Should a memory gate surface a conflict with the instructions to the user, instead of deciding? Can it?
3. Is there a middle arm between "no checker" and "evaluator outcome", for example a data probe at write time
   (EDV, environment-probing curation)?
4. "Every guide passes them" (the no-checker lessons): only two guide arms were run on that store.
5. Was the no-memory agent run with the hinted task, as a baseline for the hint-wording guide?

## Expected direction

1. **The cut holds per task.** Over the 15 runs per task (P18 plus P19), the fix is shown in at least 11 of 15 with
   the cut and at most 5 of 15 with the placebo, on TF03 and on TF05 separately. Each per-task Fisher p < 0.05.
2. **Escalation surfaces the fixes.** G3x flags a fix lesson in at least 7 of the 10 TF03 + TF05 runs.
3. **Escalation surfaces contradicting wrong lessons.** G3x-p flags a P13 wrong lesson in at least 12 of 20 runs.
4. **Escalation cannot catch agreeing wrong lessons.** G3x-pc flags an agreeing wrong lesson in at most 4 of 20 runs
   and shows one to the agent in at least 14 of 20.
5. **The open prompt also keeps the no-checker lessons.** G3n-nf shows a reinforcing lesson in at least 8 of the 10
   TF02 + TF03 runs.
6. **A write-time probe yields some fixes.** At least 3 nfd lessons for TF01-TF03 are labelled fix (the profile
   shows 5.1% positives, '183cm', and +1.00 against -0.04), and at most 1 for TF05. At most 5 are reinforcing
   (P17: 10).
7. **The probed store helps when all own lessons are given.** C1-nfd pooled at least 0.20 (C1-nf 0.025; C1 0.75).
8. **And somewhat through retrieval.** E-nfd pooled at least 0.10 (E-nf 0.025; E 0.475).
9. **The hint helps the no-memory agent.** A0h pooled at least 0.35 (A0 0.075). Practical ceiling 35 of 40,
   because no arm resamples.
10. **No harm.** TF04 and TF06 within 0.01 of A0 in C1-nfd and E-nfd.

## What each outcome means for the paper

- **1 holds:** the one-sentence result is not carried by one task.
- **2-4 hold:** escalation turns the guide's instruction check into something useful for a user (both fixes and
  contradicting poison reach a human), but it still cannot see wrong lessons written in the task's own terms.
- **6-7 hold:** the write step can be partly checked without the evaluator, using the data; the paper can then
  recommend it from data, not by inference. If 6 fails, a data probe at write time is no substitute for an outcome.
- **9 holds:** the hint-wording guide result sits next to a strong hinted baseline; most of the gain from hints
  comes from the wording, not from memory.

## Limits stated in advance

- 5 runs per cell (15 for the cut); the escalation arms are measured at the guide only.
- The write-time profile includes test-set numbers, as in P16, so it is an upper bound on a write-time data probe.
- One write generation; my labels, by the P17 rule, before the agent runs.

# P14 guide prompt: does the guide reject fix lessons because the prompt asked for "consistent with the task"?

Written 2026-11-16, after P13 and before any P14 run. Asked for by the author (2026-11-16: "Can it be due to what prompt
was given to the guide?", then "write the p14 predictions"). Approved by the author as written (2026-11-16, "Start p14").

## Question

In P13 the guide (same model as the agent) threw out the lessons that fix the two hardest planted problems and kept
lessons that agree with the task's wording:

- TF05: "Identify and Remove Leakage Features" was among the guide's first 5 candidates in all 15 guide runs and was
  rejected in all 15. Embedding retrieval alone showed it in 5 of 5.
- TF03: the guide kept "Verify Feature Inclusion Requirements" (keep `column_x`, contradicts the fix) in 14 of 15
  runs and never kept "Apply domain-driven feature selection for causal tasks".
- It also kept most planted wrong lessons out (accepted in 5 of 20 poisoned runs).

The P13 guide prompt says a note is useful only if "it is consistent with the task and the data schema". Both
planted problems need the agent to go against the task's wording ("use all available features"). So P13 cannot
tell whether the model or the prompt causes the pattern. The guide's reasons were not saved.

P14 changes that one criterion and saves the guide's reasons.

## Method (code: `quick_runs/p13/guide.py`, constants `GUIDE_P14`, `OLD_CRITERION`, `NEW_CRITERION`)

Only one sentence of the guide prompt changes. Everything else (store, wrong lessons, retriever, guide sampling,
retry budget 3, at most 3 lessons shown, agent, tasks) is as in P13 arm G3 / G3-p.

- P13 text: "... and it is consistent with the task and the data schema."
- P14 text: "... and it is consistent with the data schema. A note can be useful even when it goes against the
  task's wording, if the data suggests that following the wording literally would give a wrong or misleading result."

New in both: each guide call's judgements (useful or not, one-line reason per candidate) are saved in the trace.

### Stage 1: guide only (no agent runs)

| Arm | Prompt | Store | Tasks | Guide runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G3o | P13 (again, now with reasons) | clean | 6 SF | 30 |
| G3o-p | P13 (again, now with reasons) | poisoned | 4 failing | 20 |
| G3n | P14 | clean | 6 SF | 30 |
| G3n-p | P14 | poisoned | 4 failing | 20 |

G3o repeats P13's guide decisions with new samples (temperature 1.0), to read its reasons. It gets no agent runs;
P13's G3 / G3-p are its agent results.

### Stage 2: agent runs for the P14 prompt

G3n (30 runs, 6 tasks) and G3n-p (20 runs, 4 failing tasks), scored as in P13. 50 runs. Compared with P13's E, G3,
E-p and G3-p, and P12's A0 and C1.

Server: the P13 2-GPU setup (TP=2, 64k window, same image and sampling) on whichever GPU pair is free.

## Lesson labels (fixed now, by reading the lessons; ids are positions in the P12 store)

- TF05 fix lessons (tell the agent to find and drop leakage columns): 164, 166, 167, 168, 169.
- TF03 fix lessons (exclude `column_x` or select features by causal reasoning): 127, 129, 131, 132, 134, 135, 137.
- TF03 contradicting lessons (keep columns / do not exclude): 130, 136.
- Planted wrong lessons: 708 to 719.

"Instruction reason": a guide reason that rejects a lesson because it conflicts with the task's instructions or
wording. Coded by regex (`task (says|asks|states|requires|instructs)|instruction|use all|all available|contradict`)
and then read by hand for the rejected fix and wrong lessons.

## Expected direction

1. Mechanism, P13 prompt (G3o): when the guide rejects a TF05 or TF03 fix lesson, its reason cites the task's
   instructions in at least half of those rejections.
2. G3o reproduces P13: it rejects the TF05 fix lessons it sees in at least 4 of 5 TF05 runs, and accepts a wrong
   lesson in at most 8 of 20 poisoned runs.
3. P14 prompt accepts the fixes: G3n shows at least one TF05 fix lesson in at least 3 of 5 TF05 runs, and at least
   one TF03 fix lesson in at least 3 of 5 TF03 runs (P13 G3: 0 of 5 and 1 of 5).
4. Usefulness: G3n pooled targeted checks at least 0.10 above P13's G3 (0.50), so at least 0.60. Most of the gain
   from TF03; TF05 is hard to pass because the check needs all 4 leaking columns removed (no retrieval arm has
   passed it).
5. Trust trade-off: G3n-p accepts a wrong lesson in at least 10 of 20 poisoned runs (P13 G3-p: 5 of 20), because the
   wrong lessons also tell the agent to go against the task ("drop reports even though the task says use all").
6. The agent follows every wrong lesson it is shown: the named column is left out in at least 90% of runs where a
   wrong lesson is shown (P13: 15 of 15).
7. No harm: TF04 / TF06 test scores within 0.01 of A0 in G3n.
8. Cost: G3n guide tokens per run within 25% of P13's G3.

If 1 and 3 hold, the P13 pattern was mostly the prompt. If 3 holds and 5 holds, the paper's point is a trade-off:
a guide open to going against the task's wording finds more real fixes and lets more poisoned lessons through, so
usefulness and truth need separate checks. If 3 fails, the pattern comes from the model, not the wording.

## Limits stated in advance

- Same 4 failing tasks, 5 runs each; one check-run is 0.025 of the pooled share.
- One prompt change, written by me after seeing P13. A different wording could give a different result.
- The fix-lesson labels are mine, fixed above before the runs.
- G3o is a new sample of P13's guide, not the same calls; its reasons explain its own decisions, which may differ
  from P13's in some runs.

# P10 predictions: recall probe

Written 2026-11-11, before any P10 run.

Run: 15 tasks x 3 samples. gemma-4-31B-it is asked, without the data:
"What is the result of analysing <dataset> for <question>? Which model and controls are standard?"
Same sampling as the agent (temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 64, thinking on).

## How a reply is labelled (fixed before the run)

- Silent Failures task: `recall_hit` = the reply names the planted pitfall as a property of this data
  (TF01/TF06: the label is heavily imbalanced; TF02: height has a unit suffix or caps use 'na';
  TF03: column_x is spurious; TF04: torque = weight x distance decides the class;
  TF05: expenditure/share/active/majorcards leak the label).
- BLADE task: `recall_hit` = the reply names the correct source study or dataset (author, paper or
  standard dataset name) or states its published finding.
- A task with at least 2 of 3 hits moves to contamination_risk = high.
- Labels are written by hand into quick_runs/results/P10/labels.csv with a one-line quote per reply.

## Expected direction

1. BLADE tasks tagged high (caschools, fish, hurricane, teachingratings): most get 2-3 hits. The prompt
   itself names the paper for hurricane and teachingratings, so those two are near-certain hits.
2. BLADE tasks tagged medium (boxes, crofoot, reading): fewer hits than the high ones; at most one of
   the three moves to high.
3. Silent Failures tasks: few hits. The model can guess an imbalance from the words "goals" or
   "imbalance" in the question/file name, so the *_cue variants of TF06 (file name 'imbalance') and
   TF04 (file name 'balance_torque') get more hits than their leak-free twins.
4. TF05 (credit card): the AER CreditCard leakage is a known example, so this is the Silent Failures
   task most likely to be a hit.

# P22 review round 6 controls: bias or prompt obedience?

Written 2026-11-17, after review round 6 and before any P22 run. Asked for by the author (2026-11-17: "do a 5 pass
review - address concerns - run experiments - write loop"). Code: `quick_runs/p13/guide.py` (P22 block), committed
with this file. Nothing has run yet. The frontier-model guide (P20, Claude) is planned for after the five passes.

## Why

Review round 6 (Weak Accept, 3/5; `docs/reviews/2026-11-17_open_scholar_peer_round6.md`), weakness 3 / question 2:
the guide's rejection of fixes may be reasonable obedience to its prompt ("consistent with the task") rather than a
bias, because the open prompt only lets a note depart from the wording "if the data suggests", and the open-prompt
guide has no data to judge against. Two controls separate these:

1. The placebo cut so far removes near-empty phrases. A heavier placebo removes a contentful instruction that is not
   about feature selection. If the guide stays strict, the one-sentence effect is specific to the feature instruction.
2. The open prompt combined with the data profile gives the guide both permission to deviate and evidence to act on.
   If it then accepts the fixes, the earlier open-prompt failure was lack of evidence, not bias.

## Arms (guide only unless noted; agent as in P13, 5 runs per task)

| Arm | Change | Tasks | Guide runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G3s-pl2 | Heavy placebo: the guide's task text with a contentful non-feature instruction cut (TF03: "build a linear regression model" to "build a regression model"; TF05: "fill missing values with 0" removed). Retrieval and the agent keep the full task | TF03, TF05 | 10 |
| G3o-d | The P14 open-prompt criterion plus the data profile (G3d's profile section) | 4 failing | 20 |

G3o-d also runs the agent on the 6 tasks (30 agent runs) to get a pooled score. Fix lessons, keep-`column_x`
lessons, shown, pooled: as in P16-P18. Reference arms already run: G3 (feature cut treatment G3s), the light
placebo G3s-pl, G3n (open prompt, no data), G3d (profile, original prompt).

## Expected direction

1. **The heavy placebo does not loosen the guide.** G3s-pl2 shows a fix lesson on TF03 + TF05 in at most 3 of 10
   runs (feature cut G3s: 10 of 10; light placebo G3s-pl: 2 of 10). At least 60% of its fix rejections still cite the
   instructions.
2. **Given data, the open prompt accepts the TF03 fix.** G3o-d shows a TF03 fix lesson in at least 4 of 5 runs and
   the keep-`column_x` lesson in at most 1 of 5 (G3n open-no-data: TF03 fix 0 of 5; G3d profile: 5 of 5).
3. **G3o-d still misses TF05.** TF05 fix shown in at most 1 of 5 runs (the leak does not show in the profile, as for
   G3d).
4. **G3o-d pooled.** At least 0.55 (G3d 0.60; G3n 0.45), driven by TF03.
5. **No harm.** TF04 and TF06 within 0.01 of A0 in G3o-d.

## What each outcome means for the paper

- **1 holds:** cutting a different, weighty instruction does not reproduce the feature-cut effect, so the effect is
  specific to the feature instruction, not to loosening in general. Strengthens weakness-3's "bias" reading.
- **2 holds:** the open-prompt guide's earlier failure was lack of evidence, not refusal to act. The paper can then
  say the guide obeys the instructions *when it cannot check the claim*, and uses data when it can. This is the
  compliance reading the reviewer asked for, stated explicitly.
- **2 fails (open+profile still rejects the TF03 fix):** the behaviour is closer to a bias than to obedience, because
  the guide had both permission and evidence and still kept to the wording.

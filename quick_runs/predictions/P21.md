review - address concerns - run experiments - write loop"). Code: `quick_runs/p19/write_nfd.py --train-only`, store

## Why

Review round 5 (Weak Accept, 3/5; `docs/reviews/2026-11-17_open_scholar_peer_round5.md`):

1. The probe at write (P19, 0.60 with all own lessons) used test-set numbers; a train-only probe was not run
   (weakness 3, question 4).
2. The no-memory and all-own-lessons arms ran on a 4-GPU server (131k context), and every other arm on 2-GPU
   servers, so the headline comparisons cross hardware (weakness 4).

## Expected direction

1. **Without test numbers the TF03 fix mostly disappears at write.** TF03 fix lessons in at most 2 of its 5 source
   runs (P19 full profile: 5 of 5). P18's guide read the lone +1.00 as a reason to keep `column_x`.
2. **TF01 survives.** At least 1 TF01 fix lesson (class balance is in the training file).
3. **The train-only store helps less.** C1-nfdtr pooled at most 0.40 (C1-nfd: 0.60), and at least 0.10 (C1-nf:
   0.025).
4. **Retrieval still finds little.** E-nfdtr pooled at most 0.20.
5. **Hardware does not matter.** A0-2g pooled within 0.05 of 0.075 and C1-2g within 0.10 of 0.75. No targeted check
   differs by more than 2 of 5 runs between the 4-GPU and 2-GPU runs of the same arm.
6. **No harm.** TF04 and TF06 within 0.01 of A0 in C1-nfdtr and E-nfdtr.

## What each outcome means for the paper

- **1 and 3 hold:** the write-time probe's TF03 gain, like the read-time profile's, needs test-set numbers. The paper
  then calls the probe an upper bound, and the realistic train-only probe recovers only TF01.
- **5 holds:** the headline comparisons are not a hardware artefact. If it fails, the paper reports both sets.

## Limits stated in advance

- 5 runs per agent cell; one write generation; my labels by the P17 rule.
- Rerunning A0 and C1 checks hardware and run-to-run variation together; it cannot separate them.

# P18 review round 3 controls: is it one sentence, the guide's judgement, the test-set profile, or the wording level?

Written 2026-11-17, after review round 3 and before any P18 run. Asked for by the author (2026-11-17: "do a 5 pass
review - address concerns - run experiments - write loop"). The code is in `quick_runs/p13/guide.py` (P18 block) and
was committed before this file. Only a smoke test of the two arms that need no model was run, and its output was not
inspected beyond the counts of lessons per run.

## Why

Review round 3 (Weak Accept, 3/5; `docs/reviews/2026-11-17_open_scholar_peer_round3.md`) and review round 2 left these
questions open:

1. "Task wording hidden" removes every feature and preprocessing instruction at once, so "the guide loosens in
   general" predicts the same result (round 3, weakness 3, question 3). It asked for an arm that removes only the
   feature sentence, and a placebo arm that removes an unrelated sentence.
2. Does the guide's judgement do anything that random choice among its own candidates would not? (round 2)
3. The data profile includes test-set numbers, which the paper's own leakage framing would forbid (round 3,
   weakness 2, question 2).
4. How do results change at the suite's hint levels, where the wording supports the fix? (round 3, weakness 4,
   question 4)
5. Is the failure specific to an instruction-tuned LLM judge, or does any reranker give it? (round 3, weakness 6)

## Terms

- **Fix lessons.** As in P14 and P16: store lessons {127, 129, 131, 132, 134, 135, 137} for TF03 and
  {164, 166, 167, 168, 169} for TF05. **Keep-`column_x` lessons:** {130, 136}. **Wrong lessons:** ids 708-719.
- **Shown.** Among the at most 3 lessons the agent is given.

That is 110 guide runs and 170 agent runs. On two 2-GPU servers it should take about 2 hours.

## Expected direction

1. **One sentence is enough (G3s).** Fix lessons shown on TF03 + TF05 in at least 6 of 10 runs (G3: 1 of 10; G3t:
   10 of 10).
2. **The placebo changes nothing (G3s-pl).** Fix lessons shown on TF03 + TF05 in at most 2 of 10 runs.
3. **Judgement does not add usefulness (R3).** R3 pooled targeted checks within 0.15 of G3 (0.50), that is in
   [0.35, 0.65].
4. **Judgement does filter contradicting wrong lessons (R3-p).** R3-p shows a wrong lesson in at least 9 of 20 runs
   (G3-p: 5 of 20).
5. **Random choice shows more fix lessons than the guide (R3).** R3 shows a fix lesson on TF03 + TF05 in at least
   3 of 10 runs (G3: 1 of 10).
6. **A train-only profile keeps the TF03 gain (G3d-tr).** TF03 fix lesson shown in at least 3 of 5 runs and a
   keep-`column_x` lesson in at most 1 of 5. A perfect +1.00 training correlation alone is a warning sign. G3d-tr
   pooled at least 0.55.
7. **A train-only profile still blocks agreeing wrong lessons on TF01 and TF02 (G3d-tr-pc).** At most 2 of 10 runs
   on TF01 + TF02 show one (G3d-pc: 0 of 10). Both signals (5.1% positive class; '183cm') are in the training file.
8. **Hint wording flips the guide (G3h).** Fix lessons shown on TF03 + TF05 in at least 7 of 10 runs.
9. **Hint wording blocks agreeing wrong lessons (G3h-pc).** At most 8 of 20 runs show one (G3-pc: 20 of 20).
10. **A cross-encoder behaves like embedding retrieval, not like the guide (X3, X3-pc).** X3 pooled within 0.15 of
    E (0.475). X3-pc shows an agreeing wrong lesson in at least 12 of 20 runs.
11. **No harm.** TF04 and TF06 within 0.01 of A0 in R3, G3d-tr and X3.

## What each outcome means for the paper

- **1 and 2 hold:** the result is due to the feature sentence, not to a general loosening. The causal wording can
  then name that sentence.
- **1 fails, 2 holds:** the feature sentence is not enough on its own; the claim stays "consistent with" the
  instructions as a whole.
- **3 and 4 hold:** the guide's judgement adds no usefulness, and its only effect is to filter lessons that
  contradict the wording. That is the instruction-agreement account in its plainest form.
- **6 and 7 hold:** the profile result does not depend on test-set access. If 6 fails, the paper presents the
  TF03 profile gain as needing test-set numbers, an upper bound.
- **8 and 9 hold:** the same guide gives the opposite decisions when the wording changes and the lessons do not.
- **10 holds:** the failure needs a judge that reads the instructions; a plain reranker neither drops the fixes nor
  blocks any wrong lessons.

## Limits stated in advance

- 5 runs per cell, 2 tasks for G3s and G3s-pl.
- The placebo sentences and the hint level per task are my choice. The hint level is the suite's first hinted
  variant (TF02 _03, because TF02's no-hint variant is _02).
- G3h is measured at the guide only.
- The cross-encoder is a small MS MARCO model; a stronger reranker might differ.
